# Sales Page Skeleton

Use this section order for the draft in Step 7 of `SKILL.md`. Every
section must be written in full — no bracketed placeholders. Skip a
section only if it genuinely doesn't apply (e.g. no guarantee exists yet —
say so honestly rather than omitting it silently, and flag it in the
proof audit).

1. **Headline** — the winning headline from Step 4, plus a sub-headline
   that adds a second hook or clarifies the promise/mechanism.

2. **Lead** — first 2–4 sentences, written per the awareness stage from
   Step 2:
   - Unaware/problem-aware → story or agitation lead.
   - Solution/product-aware → mechanism or differentiation lead.
   - Most aware → direct offer or deal lead.

3. **Agitate the problem** — make the cost of inaction concrete and
   specific to the buyer description. Name what they've tried that
   hasn't worked, and why it hasn't (sets up the mechanism).

4. **Introduce the mechanism** — the specific reason this offer solves
   what others haven't. This is the "because" that makes the rest of the
   page believable (Bencivenga).

5. **Origin/credibility story** — short attractive-character framing
   (Brunson) if a founder/origin story exists in the inputs. Skip if no
   such material was supplied — do not invent a biography.

6. **The offer stack** — every component with its standalone value,
   building to the total perceived value from Step 5.

7. **Price reveal & justification** — state the price next to the value
   total and ratio. Frame the price decision, not just announce a number.

8. **Proof block(s)** — testimonials, data, case studies, credentials —
   distributed against the specific claims they support, not dumped in
   one generic block. Stack multiple proof types against the offer's
   single biggest claim (Bencivenga proof density).

9. **Objection handling / false-belief dismantling** — explicitly address
   the three beliefs from Step 6, each with its own short passage.

10. **Guarantee** — state it plainly if one was supplied. If none exists,
    do not invent one; note the gap in the proof audit instead.

11. **Urgency / scarcity** — only if real (a real deadline, real limited
    capacity, real bonus expiry). Never fabricate false scarcity
    (Kennedy's rule, and it protects long-term trust).

12. **Call to action** — one single, unambiguous next step, restated at
    every natural close point on the page (not just once at the bottom).

13. **FAQ** — answer the 3–5 real objections most likely to stop this
    specific buyer from buying, drawn from the false beliefs in Step 6
    and any "what they've tried before" detail from the buyer description.

14. **P.S.** — a closing direct-response P.S. that restates the core
    promise, the price/value gap, and the deadline or single next step.
